Self-Regulation Activity

How Would You Help Whale Feel Calm?

When a character in a book—or YOU—feels a big wave of worry or anger, circle the calming tools below that help shrink the wave!

🌬️

3 Whale Breaths

Breathe in slow through your nose, blow out through your spout!

🧸

20-Second Hug

Squeeze a parent, pillow, or favorite stuffed animal tight.

🥤

Cool Sip of Water

Take slow sips of cold water to cool down Dragon anger.

🖍️

Draw the Feeling

Put the worry on paper so it doesn't stay stuck in your head.

🐢

Turtle Cozy Corner

Curl up with a favorite picture book in a quiet spot.

🗣️

Name the Animal

Say out loud: "I feel like Impatient Fox right now!"
